            How it works and where the data comes from
            
    
              
  1. The route is rhumb lines between your positions on WGS84, as ECDIS draws them. Each time the route crosses a reporting boundary the time is worked out from the departure time and speed.
    2. 
              
  2. Report due = time at the boundary, less the notice the system asks for (for example 6 hours before entering).
    3. 
              
  3. Boundaries given as points in an IMO resolution or a national order are drawn exactly from those points. Where a system uses the territorial sea, the 12 NM limit is drawn from the Marine Regions data set (Flanders Marine Institute, CC BY 4.0); it is marked "derived, not official".
    4. 
              
  4. Every system shows its official source and the date it was checked. Rules, e-mail addresses and channels change: confirm with your latest ALRS and Notices to Mariners, and keep your corrections in the ship's notes.
